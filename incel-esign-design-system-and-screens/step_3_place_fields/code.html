COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made and effective this 24th day of May, 2024, by and between Global Property Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and Alex Rivera ("Tenant"). The Landlord hereby leases to the Tenant, and the Tenant hereby leases from the Landlord, the premises located at 123 Tech Corridor, Building B, Suite 400.

1. TERM. The term of this Lease shall be for a period of five (5) years, commencing on June 1, 2024, and ending on May 31, 2029.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay to Landlord annual rent in the amount of $48,000.00, payable in equal monthly installments of $4,000.00.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Lease as of the day and year first above written.
